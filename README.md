
# AI Small Business Toolkit

Free, open-source business tools for independent entrepreneurs and small business owners.

**Live Demo:** https://ksb7771-arch.github.io/ai-small-business-toolkit/

## About the Project

AI Small Business Toolkit is an open-source initiative focused on making practical business software accessible to entrepreneurs without dedicated engineering teams.

The project is developed by an independent entrepreneur based in South Korea with hands-on experience operating food and beverage businesses.

Our mission is simple:

**Make useful business tools accessible to everyone, regardless of technical background or company size.**

## Available Tools

### 01. Restaurant Profit Calculator

A free, browser-based financial calculator designed for restaurant owners.

Features:
- Monthly revenue and expense inputs
- Automatic operating profit calculation
- Operating profit margin analysis
- Visual cost breakdown
- Mobile-friendly interface
- No registration required
- No data uploads or external API required

**Try it here:**
https://ksb7771-arch.github.io/ai-small-business-toolkit/

## How It Works

1. Enter your monthly restaurant revenue.
2. Enter food, labor, rent, and other operating expenses.
3. View your estimated operating profit and profit margin.
4. Explore your cost breakdown.

All calculations run locally in your browser.

Results are simplified estimates and should not be treated as professional accounting advice.

## Technology

The first tool is built using:

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- GitHub Pages

No installation, backend server, or paid API is required.

How to interpret your results

Operating profit is calculated by subtracting the entered operating costs from revenue.

Operating profit margin shows how much of your revenue remains after those costs.

This is a simplified planning tool, not an accounting statement. Taxes, depreciation, interest, owner compensation, and other expenses must be included in your inputs when relevant. Results are estimates.
